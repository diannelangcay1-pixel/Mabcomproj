How can our team count participants?

To count participation across many phones, the website needs an online database and a backend service. Each device can send a minimal, anonymous event count to that service when online, while keeping journals and emotional notes on the student's device.

Offline actions can be queued and sent later, but they will not appear in the central count until the device reconnects. Counts can also be approximate because students may clear browser data or use multiple devices.

For a real school deployment: get school approval, establish a privacy notice and data-retention policy, and avoid collecting identifying information or individual mental-health details for participation analytics.

Privacy and wellbeing

MindCare is a student wellness support tool, not a confidential counseling service or a substitute for professional care. If a student feels unsafe or needs support, encourage them to reach out to a trusted adult, the school guidance counselor, or a qualified mental health professional.
