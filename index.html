Support matters

You deserve help from a real person, too.

MindCare is a wellness tool, not a therapist or emergency service. You can reach out even if you cannot explain everything clearly.

You could say: “I’ve been feeling overwhelmed lately. Can you listen without judging me and help me figure out what to do next?”

Who could I contact?

If you feel unsafe or might be in immediate danger, go to a trusted adult nearby or contact local emergency services. In the Philippines, 911 is the national emergency number. If you are at school, tell a teacher or school staff member now.
